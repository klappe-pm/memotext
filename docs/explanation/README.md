# explanation

Explanation pages give background. They are for a reader who wants to understand voicememos rather than operate it: why macOS makes the library hard to read, why the package is shaped the way it is, what it trusts and what tradeoffs it makes. They can be read away from the keyboard, they take a position, and they are the one place in the documentation where discussing alternatives is welcome.

## pages

- [where macOS keeps Voice Memos](where-macos-keeps-voice-memos.md): the folders, the files in them, how iCloud affects them, and why Full Disk Access decides whether you can read them.
- [design](design.md): why the package reads the library the way it does, why it has no dependencies and what it leaves out on purpose.
- [security model](security-model.md): what the package reads, what it never does, how it treats untrusted files and where the privacy risk sits.
